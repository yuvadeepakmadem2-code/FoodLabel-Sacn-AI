# 🥗 FoodScanAI – AI-Powered Food Analysis

**FoodScanAI** is an AI-powered food analysis platform designed to help users make healthier and more informed food choices. It simplifies the process of understanding food labels by analyzing product ingredients and nutritional information using artificial intelligence.

The platform aims to help users identify potentially unhealthy ingredients, understand food composition, and choose better alternatives based on their dietary preferences and requirements.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://scan-nourish-ai.vercel.app/

## 🚀 Features

- **AI-Powered Food Analysis:** Uses artificial intelligence to analyze food products and their ingredients.
- **Food Health Score:** Provides a simplified health score to help users understand the overall quality of a food product.
- **Ingredient Analysis:** Helps users understand ingredients present in packaged food products.
- **Personalized Food Insights:** Supports better food choices based on individual dietary needs and preferences.
- **Allergy Awareness:** Helps users identify ingredients that may conflict with their specified food allergies.
- **Nutritional Awareness:** Makes complex food label information easier to understand.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Provides a simple and intuitive experience for users.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js
- **Programming Language:** TypeScript
- **Build Tool:** Vite
- **Styling:** CSS / Tailwind CSS
- **AI Integration:** Gemini API / AI-powered analysis
- **Deployment:** Vercel

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of FoodScanAI is to make food label interpretation easier and promote healthier eating habits through artificial intelligence.

Many packaged food products contain complex ingredient lists and nutritional information that are difficult for ordinary consumers to understand. FoodScanAI addresses this problem by converting complex food information into simple insights, helping users make informed purchasing decisions.

## 💡 Why FoodScanAI?

Choosing healthy food products can be challenging because food labels often contain unfamiliar ingredients, additives, and nutritional information.

FoodScanAI bridges this information gap by providing an AI-powered solution that simplifies food analysis and helps consumers make better dietary decisions.
